SECTION 01

Cancellation Period & Timeframe

We like to keep things simple. Absolutely zero shipping costs on any products purchased through Green Thumb Depot over $200.

If you would like to cancel an order, please email or call our customer service team immediately at (833) 416-0375 to avoid return shipping fees. To submit a return - use this form.

01

Orders cancelled before we have shipped your item: Entitled to a full refund.

02

Orders that are cancelled after items have shipped: These orders will fall into our 30 Day Return policy and will be subject to return shipping costs.

SECTION 02

Damaged, Defective, and Incorrect Product Delivery

Please contact us within 24 hours for detailed return instructions. We will provide the address for returns once you have contacted us.

01

Initial return shipping fee will be covered by the customer and refunded immediately by our team upon confirmation of defective, damaged, or incorrect order being delivered.

02

Keep all initial packaging safely stored and protected so that you don't incur any restocking charges.

03

Your authorization request needs to be accepted by customer service before anything else is processed, and when approval is given, your refund will arrive within 2 business days and be paid via the same payment method used in the purchase.

SECTION 03

Product Never Arrived?

If your product has not arrived within the expected delivery time contact us immediately. You will be entitled to a full refund if for whatever reason the product is lost in transit.

If your product arrived damaged or defective you may also request a new, unblemished product. We can arrange that for you as soon as our customer service team endorses your claim. Lastly, there are some items not eligible for reimbursement if they become damaged after being used, but any entitlements under warranty remain intact.

SECTION 04

30 day return policy details

We have a 30 day return policy that starts the day your product is delivered:

25%

Products returned because of buyers remorse or change of mind will incur a 25% restocking fee and will pay the shipping fee both ways.

02

04

05

06
