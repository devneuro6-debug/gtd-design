How long does this take?

Every utility is different. Here is the typical shape of a project.

Day 1–2Eligibility review

We check your utility, facility type and equipment list against active programs.

Weeks 1–3Application and pre-approval

We prepare and submit the application. Nothing is ordered until you have approval.

Weeks 3–8Order, delivery and install

Equipment ships on your approved plan and your contractors install it.

Weeks 8–16Verification and payout

We submit final documents. The utility inspects if required, then issues your rebate.
