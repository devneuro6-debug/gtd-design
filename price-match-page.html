Our Guarantee

In the unlikely event that you find another store with the same product, (but not the same great service you've come to love) shoot us an email with the product link and we will guarantee a price match. Once we verify the pricing on the other site we will either match it or beat it! We refuse to come second place and will always make sure we have the best prices on the market.

02 / Please Read

Important Notes and Caveats

01Price must be TOTAL price and include all taxes and relevant shipping prices.
02Our price match guarantee applies pre-purchase only. We’re unable to honor price match requests or issue refunds based on lower prices found after an order has been placed.
03We only match prices from legitimate, authorized competitors. Prices advertised by known scam or unauthorized websites are not eligible.
04If a competitor is advertising below a brand’s Minimum Sale Price (MSP), we may be unable to match it. Matching prices that violate brand pricing policies could jeopardize our authorized dealer relationship.
