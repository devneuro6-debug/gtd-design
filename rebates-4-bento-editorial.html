Eligibility

Do you qualify?

Eligibility is decided by your utility and your project. These are the usual conditions.

Check my eligibility
  • You pay a commercial electric account, not a residential one.
  • The equipment is not purchased yet, since many programs need pre-approval first.
  • The equipment appears on your utility’s qualified list or meets its efficiency rules.
  • New buildouts, retrofits and expansions can all be reviewed.
  • Single rooms and multi-building campuses are both welcome.
  • Some programs limit how often one account can claim, so timing matters.

FAQ

Rebate questions, answered.

Is the rebate service really free?

Yes. The review costs nothing and carries no obligation. We earn your business only if our pricing and service make sense for your project.

Do I have to apply before I buy?

Usually. Many programs require pre-approval, and buying first can disqualify the project. Send us the plan before you place an order.

How much could I get back?

It depends on your utility, equipment and load. One six-room facility we worked with optimized $12,400 in electricity rebates. Yours may be higher or lower, and nothing is guaranteed.

Which equipment is covered?

Dehumidifiers, LED lighting, HVAC and chillers, and controls are the usual categories. Each utility publishes its own list.

How long does it take?

Program review times vary by utility. We tell you the expected timeline once we know which programs apply.

Do you do installation or engineering?

No. We provide practical equipment, sizing and rebate coordination, not stamped engineering, code compliance or on-site installation.

Can I use financing too?

Yes. Qualified projects can finance up to $500,000, and rebates and financing can be planned together.

Tell us about your project.

Ballpark is fine. We check your utility’s programs and reply the same business day, usually within a couple of hours.
